# WhatsApp Web Connection — Client Agreement

**Between:** [Your full name], trading as WaDesk ("**WaDesk**", "we")
**and:** [Client business name], [address], represented by [name, role] ("**you**")
**Plan:** [WhatsApp Web / Both] · **Monthly fee:** ₹[amount] · **Start date:** [date]
**WhatsApp number(s) to be linked:** [+91 ….]

### 1. What this connection is
WaDesk can connect your WhatsApp number by linking it as a device, the same way WhatsApp Web works on a computer.
**This is not an official WhatsApp product.** It is not approved, supported or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta. The
official alternative is the WhatsApp Business Platform ("Official API"), which WaDesk also offers.

### 2. Risks you accept
- **WhatsApp may restrict or ban your number** at any time, temporarily or permanently, for reasons it does not always
  explain — including because an unofficial connection is used.
- The connection can **disconnect**, stop working or change when WhatsApp changes its app. Messages sent or received
  during a disconnection may be delayed or lost.
- A banned or restricted number may **lose its chats, contacts and business profile** on WhatsApp.

### 3. How you will use it
- Only for **conversations with your own customers**: replying to enquiries and following up with people who contacted you.
- **No bulk messages, broadcasts, cold outreach, scraping of numbers or spam.** WaDesk blocks bulk sending on WhatsApp
  Web and limits new chats per day; you will not try to get around these limits.
- You will respect customers who ask you to stop messaging them.
- You will follow WhatsApp's terms and Indian law, including consent and data-protection rules, and you are
  responsible for the content your team sends.
- Use a number you own. We recommend a **separate business number**, not a personal one.

### 4. No liability for bans
WaDesk is **not responsible** for any ban, restriction, suspension or loss of a WhatsApp number or account, or for any
loss of messages, chats, contacts, sales or business that results from it. Fees already paid are **not refunded**
because of a ban or restriction. Apart from this, WaDesk's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees
you paid in the **last [one] month**.

### 5. Your data
Messages, contacts and files from linked numbers are stored on WaDesk's servers so your team can work with them. We use
them only to provide the service, do not sell them, and delete them within [30] days after the service ends, unless
you ask for an export before that.

### 6. Ending the service
Either side can end this agreement with [7] days' written notice (WhatsApp or email). WaDesk may pause or end the
WhatsApp Web connection at once if it is used against section 3. You can unlink the device from your phone at any time.

### 7. Acceptance
By signing, you confirm you have read and understood sections 1–6, including the risk of bans.
